## 0. Keyword ownership map

Each phrase lives on exactly one page. It appears in that page's title, H1 or H2s and in the anchors pointing at it, and nowhere else in a heading or anchor.

| Page | URL | Primary keyword | Secondary keywords | Must never target |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Christmas | `/corporate-christmas-hampers/` | corporate christmas hampers | christmas hampers for employees · christmas hampers for staff · christmas hampers for clients · corporate christmas gifts uk · luxury christmas hamper for clients · alcohol-free christmas hamper · corporate christmas gift boxes | any phrase without "christmas" or "hamper" |
| Office | `/office-snack-boxes/` | office snack boxes | office snack box · office snack subscription · office snacks delivery uk · healthy office snacks · office snack delivery · snack box for office · healthy snacks for the office | "gift", "hamper", "christmas", "wellbeing" |
| Gifting | `/corporate-orders/` | corporate snack gifts | employee gift boxes · staff wellbeing gifts · wellness gift box for employees · wellbeing gifts for staff · client gifts · new starter boxes · corporate wellbeing gifts · onboarding gift box | "office snack", "subscription", "christmas", "hamper" |

Rule of thumb: "office" and "subscription" belong to the office page. "Gift" belongs to the gifting page, unless "Christmas" or "hamper" is in the phrase, in which case it belongs to the Christmas page.

The confirmed cannibalisation pair ("office snack subscription" at 12.8 vs "snack subscription office" at 28.5) resolves because only `/office-snack-boxes/` will carry that phrase in a heading or anchor. Whichever page currently ranks 12.8 for it (check the Pages tab in Search Console, filtered to that query) keeps its content but gains a link to the office page using the anchor "office snack subscription", and drops the phrase from its own title and headings.
